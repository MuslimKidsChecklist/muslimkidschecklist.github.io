🔒 Privacy

Privacy

Last updated 20 August 2026

The short version: this app doesn't ask your child for anything. No account, no email, no name required, no sign-up. What you type stays on your own devices unless you turn on syncing, and even then it's only what you typed — never anything about who your child is.

What we collect from children

Nothing. The app never asks a child for a name, an age, a birthday, a school, a location, a photo, an email address, or a phone number. There is no sign-in and no account to create. A child opens the app and taps pictures.

What you type, and where it lives

You type the tasks and a name for each child. We recommend a nickname or a first name only — the app never requires a real name and works perfectly well with "Child 1".

That information is stored on your own device, in your browser's storage. If you turn on syncing so the same list appears on a phone and an iPad, that information is stored on our service so the devices can reach it. It is filed under a code derived from your purchase key, not under a name, an email address, or anything else that identifies you.

We never store a child's real name unless you type one. Please don't.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, ever. There are no ad networks in this app.
  • No analytics or tracking inside the app. We do not know when your child opened it or what they tapped.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of anything you type. There is nobody to sell it to.
  • No third-party code loaded into the app. It is one file with no outside requests, apart from checking whether your purchase is still valid.

Buying

Payment is handled entirely by Gumroad, who are the seller of record. Your name, email address and card details go to them, not to us — we never see or store your card. Your email address is used to send your purchase code and occasional news about our apps. You can reply to any message and ask to be removed.

The app itself never asks for your email address. Your purchase lives with Gumroad; your code lives on your device. Keeping those apart is deliberate.

Getting your information out, or deleted

Everything you typed is on your device. In the app, open Grown-ups → Save a backup to export all of it as a file you keep.

To delete it: clear the app's storage on each device, or email us and we will remove the synced copy. Because syncing is filed under a code and not a name, please send the request from the email address you bought with so we can find the right record.

Children's privacy and COPPA

This app is bought and set up by a parent or guardian. It collects no personal information from children and has no way for a child to enter any. We have designed it so there is nothing to collect.

If you believe a child has somehow entered personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so on this page and change the date at the top.
