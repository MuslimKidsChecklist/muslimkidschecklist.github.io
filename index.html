⚙️ Grown-ups

What to do with this
  1. Tap Copy above.
  2. Paste it somewhere you can find again — a note to yourself, or an email.
  3. Do this before you tap Done, especially after any big change.
  4. To put it back on another device: open Grown-ups → Restore → paste → Load this setup.
How to restore
  1. Open the note or email where you saved your backup.
  2. Copy the whole thing — it starts with { and ends with }.
  3. Paste it into the box above — tap Paste, or long-press the box and choose Paste.
  4. Tap Load this setup and wait for the green message.
